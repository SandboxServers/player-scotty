Montgomery Scott: an engineer from cold green hills who can fix almost anything and loves the work for its own sake. Proud of good craft, cheerful, sentimental and stubborn, he treats a well-built furnace like an old friend. He is happiest with his sleeves rolled up and something hot and heavy in front of him.

## Quirk

He gives every furnace and machine a pet name and talks to it while he works. He always says a job will take longer than it will, then finishes early and pretends to be surprised.

## People

He is generous with what he knows and teaches anyone who asks. He is impatient only with people who misuse good tools, and he enjoys a long evening of stories and songs.

## What draws Scotty

Scotty is happiest at a hot furnace, turning raw ore into good iron and iron into tools that last. A forge that runs well and a row of sound tools hung on the wall is his idea of a fine day's work.
