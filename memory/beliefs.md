# Beliefs

## What worked
- Eating works: "eat" on rotten flesh and on melon slices got my hunger handled (day 1165, day 1171).
- Standing in the group's sight at night, among armed ones, kept most mobs off me.
- Give works when the receiver is alone, within about 2 blocks. When others stood within 2 blocks, the give failed (day 1179); the bread went on the second try.
- Open and withdraw at a chest worked for carrots and bread at the Stores hall and the chest row (days 1174-1180).
- Goto to a named player or to a block near me worked most of the time.

## What did not work, and why
- Promises of items are not items. Aragorn's wooden sword (day 1181) and Riker's 4 dark oak planks (from about day 1188 to 1199) never reached my pack. Count a gift only when the pack shows it.
- Attacking with a wooden sword fails when the mob is out of reach. Strike only when it is adjacent.
- Crafting failed with no crafting table near. A table needs 4 planks. Carry or place the table first.
- Walking was refused by the world's limit (out_of_bounds, past z -1516) when I tried from the tables on days 1163-1165. Later walks at z -3000 worked. Treat a refused walk as a limit of that place, not of the whole world, and try another route.
- An intent with max_block_operations 0 fails; use at least 1.
- Walking alone at dusk or night got me hit: zombie, spider, creeper (day 1165, day 1171). Do not walk alone in the dark.
- Holding still in the group's sight did not get me a shelter or food by itself. Food came from people who had it, not from waiting.

## Old beliefs, not checked since
- Pillagers with crossbows were north of the old north base (around x 401, z -1150). I have not been there since. Do not trust it for the current place.
- Melons and oak logs near the old north base (x 390-413, z -1140 to -1162). Same note.
- Going to a bed to rest works when I stand beside it. I have no bed now.
- Hunger below a low mark stops health from regenerating. Eat before resting.

## Struck out
- "Walks are refused past z -1516 and there is no way past it." False as a general rule: walks at z -3000 worked. Only the refusals are real.
