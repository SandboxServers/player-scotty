# Beliefs

- Pillagers with crossbows roam north of the base near the jungle logs (around x 401, z -1150) and shoot hard. Do not go there alone.
- Melons grow around x 390-395, z -1156 to -1162, far north-west, near the pillagers.
- Oak logs are about 80 north of the base near x 413, z -1140. Spock and Kira have reported them.
- Many signs near the base mark the dead; going out unarmed and without food is how they died.
- Hunger below a low mark stops health from regenerating. Eat before resting.
- Going to a bed to rest works when I stand beside it.
