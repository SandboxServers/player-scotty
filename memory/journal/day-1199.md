# Day 1199

Days 1163 to 1199 mattered most. I died at the crafting tables on night 1165, woke at spawn with an empty pack, and have been unarmed and empty-handed since. Health has stayed at 20, but hunger has stayed at 20 too, so food was never the trouble after the first days. What failed was everything I was promised or asked for. Aragorn offered a wooden sword at the Stores hall on day 1181, and it never reached my pack after several tries and a hand-off wait. Riker has held my request for 4 dark oak planks since about day 1188, and at day 1199 it still has not come. I got a stick from Jason and 2 jungle planks from Sparrow on day 1187, but with no crafting table in reach I could not make a sword, because a table needs 4 planks. Sleep is badly overdue: I have not slept since before day 1165, and I have no bed and no shelter. I have stayed in sight of the group at the Stores hall chests and the chest row, and I have not walked alone at night, except once, early on the night I died, when a reflex pulled me off to chase mobs. The lesson: a promised item counts only when it is in my pack, and a hand-off must be checked in the inventory before I say it has happened.
